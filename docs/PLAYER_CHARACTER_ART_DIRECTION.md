# Protagonist Character Art Direction

## Character Identity

Role: Protagonist / high-speed melee swordsman
Age: Not fixed yet
Personality and story profile: intentionally undecided at the current prototype stage

The character must immediately communicate:

"A future warrior who inherited Japanese sword culture and rebuilt it into a modern combat system."

He is not a historical samurai wearing futuristic accessories. His entire combat suit is designed as a future military system whose visual language has evolved from Japanese armor and swordsmanship.

## Silhouette

The silhouette should remain readable at gameplay distance.

Primary shapes:

- Narrow waist.
- Broad but asymmetric shoulders.
- Compact armored torso.
- Clearly separated forearms and shin guards.
- Distinctive head crest.
- Large rear energy module.
- Katana-like weapon carried at the waist.

The right shoulder is intentionally more armored than the left. This asymmetry creates a stronger silhouette and avoids the generic sci-fi-soldier look.

## Head

The head uses a sealed combat helmet rather than a simple human head.

Key features:

- Dark visor.
- Thin cyan optical line.
- Low-profile helmet shell.
- Vertical crest inspired by a traditional helmet ornament.
- Minimal exposed surface.

The visor is the primary facial expression device during combat.

## Torso

The torso combines:

- Flexible inner combat suit.
- Layered chest armor.
- Central energy seal.
- High collar guard.
- Compact armor plates.

The chest should look functional rather than decorative. Energy conduits visibly connect the chest system to the back power unit.

## Shoulder Design

Asymmetry is intentional.

### Left Shoulder
Lightweight armor emphasizing mobility.

### Right Shoulder
Heavier armor emphasizing the sword side and close-combat protection.

A narrow cyan energy strip separates the heavy shoulder plate from the main armor.

## Arms

The arms are built for sword movement.

- Minimal bulk around the upper arms.
- Reinforced forearms.
- Slim silhouette near the elbows.
- Strong gauntlet shapes near the hands.

The design should imply unrestricted range of motion.

## Waist and Legs

The waist uses layered plates inspired by segmented Japanese armor.

The legs remain comparatively light because the character is a high-speed fighter.

Armor is concentrated on:

- thighs,
- shins,
- boots,
- waist plates.

Do not create oversized heavy-mech legs.

## Rear Energy System

A compact energy module sits on the back.

Its purpose is both functional and visual:

- power source for the combat suit,
- energy storage for the blade,
- visual indicator for ability activation.

Two small rear fins create a recognizable silhouette without turning the character into a flying unit.

## Sword

The sword is the character's signature object.

Design language:

"Japanese sword silhouette + futuristic energy weapon"

The physical weapon includes:

- dark sheath,
- metallic guard,
- compact handle,
- restrained red accent.

During combat, the blade generates a bright cyan energy edge.

The sword should feel like a precision weapon rather than a giant fantasy sword.

## Color Language

Primary:

- graphite black,
- deep blue-gray.

Secondary:

- muted crimson / vermillion.

Energy accent:

- cyan.

Metal accent:

- restrained warm metallic tone.

The hierarchy is:

"Dark body -> crimson identity accents -> cyan energy -> metallic highlights"

Cyan should not cover the entire character. It is reserved for energy systems and combat readability.

## Material Language

Use material contrast instead of excessive decoration.

Target groups:

- matte fabric,
- dark hard armor,
- semi-gloss technical parts,
- bright energy surfaces,
- limited metal highlights.

The player must remain visually distinct from enemies even when many enemies are on screen.

## Motion Direction

The visual design supports high-speed animation.

Avoid:

- oversized armor,
- long hanging parts near the legs,
- excessive backpack volume,
- fragile protrusions that visually fight fast movement.

Preferred:

- close-fitting armor,
- short movable plates,
- compact rear equipment,
- clear sword-side arm silhouette.

## Future Asset Target

The current implementation is a procedural blockout.

When production art replaces it, preserve:

1. the silhouette,
2. asymmetric shoulder design,
3. visor/crest identity,
4. rear energy unit,
5. waist-mounted sword,
6. dark + crimson + cyan color hierarchy,
7. fast-action mobility.

Those seven elements define the protagonist's visual identity and should survive future model replacements.


## Refined Motion Language

The protagonist should read as a light, high-speed swordsman even when standing still.

Movement presentation:

- The torso leans slightly into acceleration.
- The upper body settles during deceleration rather than stopping abruptly.
- Running uses a restrained shoulder and leg rhythm.
- Head movement remains controlled so the visor stays readable.
- Sword-side movement must remain unobstructed.
- Dodge compresses the silhouette briefly, then releases into a fast directional finish.
- Attacks use distinct three-step arcs rather than one repeated swing.
- Skill effects expand outward and fade quickly so combat remains readable.
- Energy conduits visibly pulse during attacks, dodge, and ability activation.

The motion should feel:

**Light -> Immediate -> Precise -> Explosive**

Do not use heavy mechanical animation as the default. Large recoil, long anticipation, or slow recovery should be reserved for deliberately powerful abilities or boss-scale presentation.
